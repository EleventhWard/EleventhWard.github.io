    Approach

    Grids, Grids, Grids.
    Grids are a vital part of Swiss design since they provide structure, balance, and consistency. They assist in aligning text, images, and other elements, which in turn leads to clean and well-organised layouts that are simple to navigate. Through the establishment of a clear visual hierarchy, grids enable designers to communicate information efficiently without compromising on simplicity and clarity.
